# Code of Conduct

Tomelight is built by and for people who want their tools to be calm, clear and useful. We want the project space to feel the same way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most confusion is a missing detail, not bad faith.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. "This breaks on files with Windows line endings" helps. "This is bad" doesn't.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone asks a first question once.
- Respect people's time. Search before opening an issue, and keep threads on topic.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or trolling.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules. For anything serious, report it privately: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability** (it reaches the maintainers only, and works for conduct reports too). Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any official Tomelight channel.
